About me

As of the year 2024, I'm an assistant professor at BIMSA.

I obtained my math PhD at UC Berkeley in 2018, advised by Vivek Shende and Richard E. Borcherds.

My research interests include microlocal sheaf theory and Legendrian knots, Hodge theory of character varieties and P=W, and mirror symmetry.
